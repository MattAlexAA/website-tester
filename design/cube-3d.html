How the model works

One cube, four letters

A real 5×5×5 cube. Each side carries one cube of the logo: A on the front in red, S in blue on the right, A in green on the back, P in orange on the left. A ? sits on top in yellow and a black ! on white underneath. Turning a slice pulls the next letter round.

Inverted look

The letter tiles are white and the rest of each face is the cube colour, on black plastic like a real puzzle.

Rotate and turn

Click and drag anywhere to rotate the whole cube, all the way round, and flick it to spin. Hover a tile to turn its slice. On a phone, drag the background to rotate and swipe across tiles to turn them. Arrow keys rotate it too.

Solves itself

After a second without a turn, it undoes every move in reverse until the logo is back.
