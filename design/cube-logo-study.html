What changes

Inverted look

The grid lines and frame take the cube's colour instead of black, so the white tiles read as one solid letter: A, S, A, P.

The turn

A row or column of five tiles rotates in 3D like a cube slice, bringing in the next face. Three turns scramble it, three turns solve it. About a second in total.

Where it goes

Header logo, hero cubes, closing row, cube page badges, comparison table and footer. It replaces the current yellow flip.

Reduced motion

Visitors who've switched off animation on their device see the static logo with no turns.
