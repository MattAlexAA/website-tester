How it works

Here is how working with ASAP Assets runs, from the first call to finished videos.

The steps

  1. Book a 30-minute call.
  2. We send a proposal. Take your time to review it.
  3. Sign the agreement.
  4. Pay a 50% deposit to start.
  5. Your concepts and shoot guides are delivered: all 40 within 7 days, or in batches of 10.
  6. The 50% balance is due on concept delivery. On the batch option, the balance is due with the second batch.
  7. You film using the shoot guides and send your footage through a shared Google Drive folder.
  8. On Middle, Premium and Accelerated Growth, we edit your footage: a batch of 10 videos in 5–7 days, or a full 40 in about 2 weeks.
  9. On Premium only, we schedule, post and run your Meta ads.

Two ways to receive your 40 concepts

  • All 40 at once, within 7 days.
  • Four batches of 10, with the first batch within a couple of days and the rest on an agreed timeline, so filming feels less daunting.

What a shoot day guide contains

Each concept includes the concept name and length, a 2–3 sentence positioning summary, a shot-by-shot plan with timings and voiceover lines, the on-screen hook, a caption line and the post caption. Guides group concepts that can be filmed together in one setup.

An example concept

This is an anonymised entry from a real client guide, written for a Melbourne sports and health clinic.

Concept 8: Car Boot to First Tee (35s)

Positioning: Golfers go straight from the car to a full swing. A 60-second follow-along warm-up filmed on the footpath outside the clinic: saveable, practical, with a summer hook.

  • Shot 1 (0–3s): The clinician lifts a golf bag out of a car boot and goes straight into a full swing. Voiceover: "Straight out of the car and into a full swing? Do this first."
  • Shot 2 (3–10s): Club held along the spine, hinging at the hips. Voiceover: "One. Club along your back. Hinge at the hips, ten times."
  • Shot 3 (10–17s): Club across the shoulders, slow rotations. Voiceover: "Two. Slow turns each way, let your hips come with you."
  • Shot 4 (17–24s): Lunge and turn toward the front leg, swap sides. Voiceover: "Three. Step into a lunge and turn. Swap sides."
  • Shot 5 (24–35s): Half swings, then a full swing, turn to camera. Voiceover: "Sixty seconds. Tag the mate who tees off straight out of the car."

On-screen hook: Do this before your first tee shot

Caption line: Quick golf warm up before tee off

Can I just use ChatGPT for content ideas?

Yes, AI tools are great for brainstorming. Where they fall short is knowing your brand, your audience, your local market and what's actually working in your industry, then turning ideas into a shoot plan you can film in a day. ASAP Assets gives you that, refined by marketers with five years of big-brand experience, for less than a typical agency.

What are AI tools good at for content ideas?

AI tools such as ChatGPT are fast. They are useful for brainstorming a long list of ideas, getting unstuck when you don't know what to post, and drafting variations of a hook or caption. For an owner with time to refine and plan, that is a good starting point.

Where do AI tools fall short?

AI tools fall short in four places: brand context, local market knowledge, shoot planning, and consistency over 3 months. They give fast generic ideas with no shoot plan and no brand or local market context.

  • Brand context. An AI tool doesn't know your brand, your audience or how you sound unless you explain it every time.
  • Local market. It doesn't know your area, your customers or what's actually working in your industry.
  • Shoot planning. A list of ideas is not a shoot plan. You still need a shot-by-shot plan with timings, voiceover lines, hooks and captions, grouped so you can film in a day.
  • Consistency over 3 months. One good idea is easy. Holding one brand story together across a full quarter of posts is the hard part.

When is doing it yourself enough?

If you love marketing, have hours to spare and are happy to refine the ideas and plan the filming yourself, AI tools plus your own time can be enough. You keep full control, at the cost of your time.

When should I bring in ASAP Assets?

When you want a plan and premium-looking content without boutique agency pricing. Small Business packages include 40 concepts plus shoot day guides within 7 days, with optional editing, scheduling and Meta ads, refined by marketers with five years of big-brand experience.
